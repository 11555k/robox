Embedded Systems Training

Comprehensive courses in embedded systems design and development, covering microcontrollers, real-time systems, and hardware-software integration.

Embedded Systems & Microcontrollers

Our embedded systems training program provides students with hands-on experience in designing and programming microcontroller-based systems. This course is essential for students pursuing careers in electronics, automation, and IoT development.

  • Microcontroller architecture and programming (Arduino, ARM, PIC)
  • Real-time operating systems and task scheduling
  • Interfacing with sensors, actuators, and communication modules
  • Hardware-software co-design principles
  • Debugging and testing embedded systems
  • IoT and wireless communication integration

Electronics Training

Electronics & Circuit Design

Our electronics training program covers everything from basic circuit theory to advanced PCB design and manufacturing. Perfect for schools looking to establish or enhance their electronics laboratories with practical, hands-on learning experiences.

  • Component selection and circuit optimization
  • Prototyping and testing methodologies
  • Power electronics and motor control systems
