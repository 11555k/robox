Education Kits

Comprehensive educational kits designed to teach college students logic design and digital electronics fundamentals. Our kits provide hands-on learning experiences that bridge theory and practical application.

NAND Gates Kit

This educational kit is specifically aimed at teaching college students logic design, with a focus on NAND gates and fundamental digital circuit principles. The kit includes all necessary components, breadboards, and comprehensive learning materials that guide students through building and understanding various logic gate configurations. Perfect for laboratory sessions and coursework, it helps students grasp the foundational concepts of digital electronics through hands-on experimentation and practical problem-solving exercises.

Robotics & Innovation Project Kit

This comprehensive project kit is aimed at teaching students in a more physical and hands-on way, specifically designed for robotics and innovation laboratories. The kit helps students solve real-world problems using a combination of electronics and mechanical designs. It includes components for building functional robots, sensors for environmental interaction, microcontrollers for programming, and mechanical parts for structural assembly. Perfect for innovation labs, this kit encourages creativity, critical thinking, and practical engineering skills while helping students understand how to integrate electronic systems with mechanical components to create working solutions.
